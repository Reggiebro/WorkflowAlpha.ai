What is Mentary AI

Mentary AI is a fully self-contained, autonomous local AI model designed for organizations and individuals who cannot risk exposing sensitive data to external services. It runs natively on your own hardware — no cloud, no API calls, no telemetry, no data leaving your environment.

Who it's for: security-conscious businesses, healthcare providers handling PHI, law firms managing privileged communications, government agencies with strict data sovereignty requirements, financial institutions, and anyone who processes sensitive data and cannot rely on external AI services.

Unlike cloud-based AI tools that send your prompts and data to remote servers, Mentary AI operates entirely within your infrastructure. You retain complete data sovereignty — every query, every response, every byte stays under your control.

Why Mentary AI

Complete data sovereignty. Zero external dependencies. Built for environments where privacy isn't optional — it's mandatory.

🧠 Fully Self-Contained AI Model

Mentary AI ships with all model weights, inference engine, and runtime dependencies bundled together. There are no external API calls, no server-side processing, and no hidden network activity. The model performs all computation locally on your hardware, ensuring consistent availability even in fully air-gapped environments.

🔌 Zero External Network Reliance

Once deployed, Mentary AI requires absolutely no internet connectivity to function. It performs inference entirely using local compute resources — CPU, GPU, or both. This eliminates the risk of data interception, man-in-the-middle attacks, and unauthorized telemetry transmission that plague cloud-based AI solutions.

🔒 Total Data Privacy by Design

Every query you submit and every response generated stays within your environment. There are no logs sent to external servers, no usage analytics collected, and no third-party access of any kind. For healthcare, legal, and government use cases, this architecture simplifies compliance with HIPAA, GDPR, and data residency regulations.

⚙️ Custom Deployment Included

Your license includes deployment engineering support tailored to your infrastructure. Whether you need bare-metal installation, containerized deployment via Docker, or integration with existing on-premise systems, our team ensures Mentary AI runs optimally in your environment — including air-gapped and high-security configurations.
