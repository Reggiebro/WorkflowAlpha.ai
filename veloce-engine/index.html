Veloce-Engine is a complete B2B platform built specifically for food & beverage operations. Everything your operation needs, in one platform

Four core systems working together. No third-party integrations required, no per-seat pricing, no monthly subscriptions. One license covers your entire operation.

📋 Reservation Management

Full reservation lifecycle from booking to table turn. Walk-ins, online bookings, phone reservations — all managed from a single view. Table assignment, waitlist management, and no-show tracking built in. Your front-of-house team sees everything in real time, so double-bookings become impossible.

💬 Real-Time Staff Communication

Internal messaging that works across the entire operation. Send updates to individuals, roles (kitchen, bar, front-of-house), or the whole team. No personal phone numbers required — staff communicate through the platform. Shift swaps, schedule changes, and urgent announcements reach everyone instantly.

🔄 Automated Shift Planning

Build schedules in minutes, not hours. The system considers availability, role requirements, and staffing levels to suggest optimal schedules. One-click publishing notifies all affected staff automatically. Shift swaps and time-off requests flow through an approval pipeline — no more text-message scheduling chaos.
